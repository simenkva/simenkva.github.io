Research profile

Theoretical and computational quantum chemistry, with emphasis on coupled-cluster theory, density-functional theory, quantum dynamics, and mathematical and numerical analysis of electronic-structure methods. Research at the interface of chemistry, physics, applied mathematics, and quantum computing.
